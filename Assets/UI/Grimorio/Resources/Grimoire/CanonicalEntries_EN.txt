@A01|On Melantha
>TEXT
Melantha.
Daughter of the night, apprentice of forgetting.
For as long as I can remember, dreams have always felt more real than the days.
Maybe that is why I started writing.
Not to remember everything.
To notice when something disappears.
This grimoire keeps what I can still name:
what I was,
what I am
and, perhaps, what I may still be.
May I not forget myself.
>NOTE
Dreams leave traces too.
@A02|The Bedroom
>TEXT
I know every board of this floor.
Even so, sometimes I wake up here as if I had been placed in someone else's room.
The bed.
The writing desk.
The window.
The circle.
Everything is where it should be.
That is the problem.
When something is wrong, the room does not change its shape.
It changes its meaning.
>NOTE
A house can also forget who lives in it.
@A03|Poe
>TEXT
Poe shows up before I finish deciding whether I want company.
He does not ask.
He just stays.
Sometimes he watches me as if he knew what I was looking for before I did.
It is irritating.
It is also comforting.
I do not remember when I started trusting him so much.
Maybe I should have written it down.
>NOTE
He hates it when I pretend I did not hear him.
@A04|The Circle
>TEXT
I know these symbols.
Not all of them by name, but by gesture.
Hands remember things the mind loses.
The circle does not look destroyed.
It looks interrupted.
The four candles were left as points of support.
If I can rebuild the order, maybe I will find out what I was trying to keep here.
@A05|The Labyrinth
>TEXT
It does not look built.
It looks remembered.
The corridors fit together like thoughts trying to form a story after important parts were torn out.
There are doors that return you to the same place.
Voices that know things I never said.
Paths that seem to change when they notice I am paying attention.
If this place wants something from me, it has not decided how to ask yet.
>NOTE
Do not follow a wall.
Follow what changes.
@A06|Andrealphus
>TEXT
The name was in the grimoire before I remembered the face.
That does not reassure me.
Andrealphus speaks like someone who already knows how a conversation ends.
He answers questions with other questions.
When he seems to be lying, I can never tell which part of the sentence is the lie.
Maybe that is the point.
>NOTE
Knowledge can also be a form of threat.
@A07|The Seven Domains
>TEXT
Seven paths.
Seven Arcana.
The Moon.
The Tower.
The Hanged Man.
The Hermit.
The Devil.
Death.
Judgement.
They do not look like doors in the usual sense.
They look like different ways of crossing the same thing.
Six remain closed.
The Moon answered.
For now, that is enough.
>NOTE
Every door also chooses what it leaves outside.
@A08|The Moon
>TEXT
The Moon never shows everything.
Maybe that is why this place feels so familiar.
Reflections.
Phases.
Cycles.
Things that disappear without ceasing to exist.
Here, looking straight at something seems to be the easiest way not to see it.
I must pay attention to what changes between one image and the next.
>NOTE
Absence is not the same as emptiness.
@A09|Lunar Garden
>TEXT
The flowers here do not grow toward the sun.
They obey the Moon.
Waxing.
Full.
Waning.
New.
Some respond to presence.
Others to reflection.
Others only to the passing of a full cycle.
I must not treat them as ordinary flowers.
>NOTE
Flowers know how to wait too.
@A10|Reflections
>TEXT
An ordinary mirror repeats.
These ones interpret.
One shows the instant.
Another arrives late.
Another seems to already know what I will do.
Some remove things from the image.
Others add to it.
The most unsettling thing is not when the reflection is wrong.
It is when it seems more certain of itself than I am.
>NOTE
Not every reflection belongs to the present.
@A11|Fragment
>TEXT
It does not look broken.
It looks incomplete.
There is a difference.
When I touched it, I did not feel I was finding something new.
I felt something recognized me.
I do not know what it is.
I also do not know why the idea of leaving it here feels impossible.
I will carry it with me.
Until I understand.
>NOTE
Some things weigh more when we do not know who they belong to.
@A12|Return
>TEXT
I am back.
The sentence should be enough.
It is not.
The room is in the same place.
So is the bed.
The circle has gone out.
I came back.
But some part of the question came back with me.
Maybe that is what the Fragment is.
Not an answer.
A direction.
>NOTE
First Domain: The Moon.
@R01|Principles of a Circle
>TEXT
A circle is not only for containing.
It determines where one thing begins and where another loses its authority.
Before any operation:
observe the boundary;
recognize the points;
determine the intention;
do not interrupt the order.
An incomplete operation does not disappear.
It remains open.
>NOTE
Never sleep inside an active circle.
@R02|The Four Flames
>TEXT
Four flames sustain this operation.
They must not be lit according to their position.
Each one corresponds to a meaning.
The order belongs to the symbol, not to the place.
It begins where the light wanes.
It continues in that which records.
It passes through that which preserves a face.
It ends where the body rests.
Only then may the center be touched.
>NOTE
Moon.
Word.
Memory.
Rest.
@R03|On Thresholds
>TEXT
Doors are obvious thresholds.
Windows are more dangerous.
A door asks whether you want to pass.
A window lets two things watch each other without either crossing.
During an operation, the boundary must remain stable.
Wind, reflection and outside light can alter a flame.
>NOTE
Closing does not always mean preventing.
Sometimes it means sustaining.
@R04|Names
>TEXT
Naming something does not mean understanding it.
But it makes it possible to call it again.
Names create direction.
That is why they must be used with care.
What answers may not be what was called.
>NOTE
Write first.
Speak afterwards.
@R05|Echoes
>TEXT
An ordinary echo repeats the shape of a voice.
A ritual echo repeats the intention.
If the answer changes, it does not necessarily mean the question changed.
Maybe the one listening has changed.
Do not answer the word.
Answer the meaning.
@R06|Ritual Mirrors
>TEXT
Reflections may record:
present;
delay;
anticipation;
absence;
duplication;
space.
None of them is true on its own.
In an operation of memory, look for relations.
Do not look for the most perfect image.
Look for the images that reveal time and place.
@R07|Fragmented Sigil
>TEXT
A broken sigil must not be rebuilt by its appearance.
First:
recognize the phase.
Then:
recognize the record.
Finally:
determine the intention.
Phase.
Memory.
Intention.
When the three agree, the shape stops resisting.
>NOTE
The symbol does not obey the hand.
It obeys the meaning.
